06 / Strategic Paths

The Sequencing Question

01

Hiring Platform First

Build the hiring venture as the core bet. The agent matching state engine developed becomes the foundation for a generalized managed platform later, leveraging a clear campus distribution wedge and high contract values.

02

Agent Platform First

Deploy as a productized NULLPOINT retainer service immediately. Higher speed to first cash flow, but operates against a narrowing competitive window and features a significantly lower long-term valuations ceiling.

03

Both Simultaneously

Run both projects concurrently to maintain options. Stretches a two-person team exceptionally thin and increases risk of neither achieving escape velocity.
